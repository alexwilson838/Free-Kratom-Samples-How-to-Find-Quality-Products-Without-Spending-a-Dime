## Frequently Asked Questions About Free Kratom Samples

### Are free kratom samples actually free?

Most free kratom samples require covering shipping costs, typically ranging from $3-10. Some vendors include samples free with qualifying purchases, while others offer completely free samples during special promotions. "Free" generally refers to product cost rather than total transaction cost.

### How much kratom is in a typical free sample?

Standard free kratom samples contain 2-5 grams of powder, sufficient for one to three servings depending on individual dosing preferences. Some promotional programs offer larger quantities up to 10-25 grams.

### Can beginners request free kratom samples?

Yes, beginners represent ideal candidates for free sample programs. Samples allow newcomers to explore different strains and assess personal responses before committing to larger purchases. However, beginners should research proper usage, start with conservative amounts, and consult healthcare providers.

### How often can someone request free kratom samples?

Vendor policies vary significantly. Some allow monthly sample requests, others limit samples to first-time customers, and some have no explicit restrictions. Ethical practice involves respecting stated policies and avoiding program abuse through multiple accounts or excessive requests.

### Do free kratom samples expire?

Kratom maintains potency for 1-3 years when stored properly in cool, dark, dry conditions. Free samples should include packaging dates or best-by information. Older samples may have reduced potency but generally remain safe for consumption if stored correctly and showing no signs of contamination.

### What's the catch with free kratom samples?

Legitimate vendors offer samples as marketing investments to acquire customers and demonstrate product quality. The "catch" involves modest shipping costs and vendor hopes that satisfied sample recipients convert to paying customers. Reputable vendors don't employ hidden fees, automatic subscriptions, or deceptive practices.

### Are free kratom samples lab tested?

Quality vendors maintain consistent lab testing standards across all products, including free samples. However, smaller or less reputable vendors might skip testing for sample batches. Always verify lab testing availability and request current certificates of analysis before consuming samples.

### Can free kratom samples be shipped internationally?

International shipping for free kratom samples is rare due to high costs, complex regulations, and legal restrictions in many countries. Most sample programs serve domestic customers only. International kratom enthusiasts should research local vendors or specialty import services.
